Overview

KESMO creates practical web tools designed to be simple, transparent, and respectful of user privacy.

The KESMO Starter Kit is a static website foundation. By default, it does not require user accounts, collect information through forms, store personal information, or transmit tool inputs to KESMO.

Individual KESMO tools may use different features or third-party services. Each published project must update its privacy policy so it accurately describes what that specific tool does.

Information processed locally

KESMO tools may allow users to enter values, select options, or provide other information needed to produce a result.

Unless a tool clearly states otherwise, this information is processed locally in the user’s web browser. Local processing means the information is used on the user’s device and is not sent to KESMO.

Users should avoid entering confidential, sensitive, or personally identifying information into a tool unless the tool specifically explains why that information is required and how it will be handled.

Information collected automatically

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most website-hosting services, GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver the site, maintain security, and operate its services.

This technical information may include details such as an Internet Protocol address, browser type, device type, operating system, requested pages, and access times.

KESMO does not receive direct access to GitHub’s private server logs through the Starter Kit.

Cookies and local storage

The KESMO Starter Kit does not set cookies or use browser storage by default.

A future KESMO tool may use local storage to remember a user preference or preserve non-sensitive settings on the user’s device. When local storage is used, the project’s privacy policy should explain what is stored and why.

Third-party services added to a project may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Analytics

The KESMO Starter Kit does not include analytics by default.

Individual KESMO projects may later use privacy-conscious analytics to understand general usage, identify technical problems, and improve the user experience.

Any project that adds analytics must update this policy before publication and identify the analytics provider and the categories of information processed.

Advertising

The KESMO Starter Kit does not include advertising by default.

Some individual KESMO tools may display advertising to support free public access. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, personalize, limit, or measure advertisements.

Any project that adds advertising must update its privacy policy before publication and identify the advertising provider and applicable user choices.

External links and services

KESMO websites may contain links to external websites, repositories, platforms, or services that are not controlled by KESMO.

KESMO is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, availability, or content of external services. Users should review the policies of those services before providing information or relying on them.

Children’s privacy

The KESMO Starter Kit is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Individual tools intended for children or likely to be used by children require additional review before publication to ensure their features, content, data practices, and policies are appropriate.

Security

KESMO reduces privacy and security risk by limiting data collection and preferring local browser-based processing whenever practical.

No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should not submit sensitive information through a KESMO tool unless the tool clearly supports and explains that use.

Changes to this policy

KESMO may update this privacy policy when the Starter Kit, a project’s features, legal requirements, or third-party services change.

Material updates should be reflected in the “Last updated” date near the top of this page and documented in the project’s version history when appropriate.
