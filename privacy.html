Overview

KESMO creates practical web tools designed to be simple, transparent, and respectful of user privacy.

The KESMO website is a static website that serves as the central directory for published KESMO web tools. It does not require user accounts, collect information through forms, store personal information, or process tool inputs.

Individual KESMO tools may use different features or third-party services. Each published tool maintains its own privacy policy describing the specific features, information practices, and services used by that tool.

Information processed locally

Some KESMO tools may allow users to enter values, select options, or provide other information needed to produce a result.

Unless a tool clearly states otherwise, this information is processed locally in the user’s web browser. Local processing means the information is used on the user’s device and is not sent to KESMO.

Users should avoid entering confidential, sensitive, or personally identifying information into a KESMO tool unless the tool specifically explains why that information is required and how it will be handled.

Information collected automatically

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most website-hosting services, GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver the site, maintain security, and operate its services.

This technical information may include details such as an Internet Protocol address, browser type, device type, operating system, requested pages, and access times.

KESMO does not receive direct access to GitHub’s private server logs through this website.

Cookies and local storage

The KESMO website does not set cookies or use browser storage.

Individual KESMO tools may use local storage to remember user preferences or preserve non-sensitive settings on the user’s device. When local storage is used, the tool’s privacy policy should explain what is stored and why.

Third-party services used by individual tools may set cookies, use local storage, or use similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

Analytics

The KESMO website does not currently include analytics.

KESMO may later use privacy-conscious analytics to understand general website usage, identify technical problems, and improve the user experience.

If analytics are added, this policy will be updated to identify the provider and explain the categories of information processed.

Advertising

The KESMO website does not currently display advertising.

Some individual KESMO tools may display advertising to support free public access. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, personalize, limit, or measure advertisements.

Any KESMO tool that displays advertising should identify the advertising provider and explain applicable privacy choices in its own privacy policy.

External links and services

The KESMO website contains links to individual KESMO tools and may also link to external websites, repositories, platforms, or services.

External websites and services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. KESMO is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, availability, or content of services outside its control.

Users should review the policies of external services before providing information or relying on them.

Children’s privacy

The KESMO website is not designed to collect personal information from children.

KESMO tools intended for children, or likely to be used by children, require additional review before publication to ensure their content, features, data practices, and policies are appropriate.

Security

KESMO reduces privacy and security risk by limiting data collection and preferring local browser-based processing whenever practical.

No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should not submit sensitive information through a KESMO tool unless that tool clearly supports and explains that use.

Changes to this policy

KESMO may update this Privacy Policy as the website, its features, legal requirements, or third-party services change.

Material updates will be reflected in the “Last updated” date near the top of this page.
